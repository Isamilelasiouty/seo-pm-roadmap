Architecture Crucial Distinction: Functional Prototype vs. Production System

In Product Management, you must clearly distinguish between a validated prototype (which proves market demand and core problem-solving logic) and a scalable production product (which handles enterprise reliability, security, and edge cases).

Dimension Your Capstone MVP (Functional Prototype) Full Enterprise Production System

The 12 Required Portfolio Artifacts

Click each artifact checkbox as you complete and verify it. These form the core of your PM transition portfolio.

0 of 12 Artifacts Completed
1
Problem Statement & Target User Definition
A 1-page executive brief framing the exact pain point: why manual GSC traffic-drop diagnosis is slow, error-prone, and causes misaligned fixes.
Required Elements: Context, Trigger Event, Current Workaround, Quantified Cost of the Problem, Target Persona (e.g., Agency SEO Lead vs In-House Organic Growth PM).
2
5 SEO Practitioner Interview Transcripts & Notes
Field research notes from 5 distinct 20-minute user discovery interviews conducted with practicing SEO specialists.
Required Elements: Semi-structured script following "The Mom Test", verbatim quotes, timestamped friction points, and zero hypothetical leading questions.
3
Synthesized & Ranked Pain Points Matrix
An affinity map and frequency matrix categorizing all observed practitioner frustrations into actionable problem clusters.
Required Elements: Severity rating (1–5), Frequency of mention, Current emotional frustration, and Impact on weekly billable hours.
4
MVP Scope Definition & "Out-of-Scope" Boundary
A rigorous MoSCoW document establishing the exact boundaries of the AI diagnostic tool version 1.0.
Required Elements: Must-Have features (GSC anomaly parsing, pattern clustering), and explicitly excluded Won't-Have features (no backlink crawlers, no spam generators).
5
GSC & Supplementary Data Requirements Spec
Technical schema defining every data field ingested by the diagnostic engine, along with validation and cleaning rules.
Required Elements: Fields (Date, Page, Query, Clicks, Impressions, CTR, Position), data freshness constraints (48-hr lag), anonymized query threshold handling.
6
Root-Cause Diagnostic Decision Framework
A deterministic flowchart / decision tree mapping how the tool moves from symptom to diagnosis.
Required Elements: Branching logic for Seasonal Dips, Google Core Updates, SERP Feature Cannibalization (AI Overviews), and Technical De-indexing.
7
Diagnostic Results Screen Wireframe & UI Spec
Visual interface blueprint (Figma or detailed schematic) of the core user experience screen where findings are revealed.
Required Elements: Progressive disclosure hierarchy, evidence visualization, confidence rating badge, actionable next steps, and error/empty states.
8
Lightweight Product Requirements Document (PRD)
The master technical document for engineers containing user stories, edge cases, and non-functional requirements.
Required Elements: 4 User Stories in Given/When/Then acceptance syntax, Definition of Done (DoD), performance SLAs (<15s diagnosis), and error boundaries.
9
KPI Framework & Measurement Plan
A complete product analytics architecture defining how you will measure product success and prevent vanity metrics.
Required Elements: North Star Metric ("Weekly Resolved Diagnoses"), Input Metrics (Time-to-Diagnosis, Acceptance Rate), and Guardrail Metrics.
10
Historical Case Validation & Backtesting Plan
The scientific validation protocol for proving the AI engine's diagnostic accuracy against historical search drop cases.
Required Elements: 10 Benchmark case datasets with verified root causes, Precision & Recall calculation methodology, and criteria for AI prompt refactoring.
11
Risk Register & Trust/Safety Mitigation Matrix
A proactive risk document evaluating every point of failure for an AI-powered diagnostic product.
Required Elements: Data Privacy (sanitizing URLs), Hallucination Risks (guardrails against false technical advice), and User Trust mechanisms.
12
Executive Product Portfolio Case Study
The culminating centerpiece artifact: a structured, beautifully formatted case study ready to present in PM job interviews.
Required Elements: Executive Summary, Problem Space, Discovery Insights, Prioritization Math, Technical PRD, Validation Results, and Lessons Learned.
