Root Cause Analysis, 5 Whys & Traffic Drop Investigation
Competency 1 | 6.5 Estimated Study Hours
0/4 Done
Learning Objectives
  • Diagnose the geometric pattern of a traffic decline (sudden cliff drop vs gradual slope drop vs seasonal trend).
  • Apply structured root cause analysis frameworks (5 Whys, Ishikawa diagrams) to complex organic drops.
  • Cross-reference Search Console data with Google Search Status Dashboard updates and GA4 behavioral paths.
Core Concepts
  • Drop Patterns: Cliff drop (Technical/Manual Action) vs Gradual decline (Content decay/Competition).
  • The 5 Whys Methodology: Iterative root-cause inquiry to push past superficial symptoms.
  • Ishikawa Mapping: Organizing causes by Technical, Algorithmic, Content, and Competitive.

Week 8
Hypothesis Generation & Falsifiable Testing Design
Competency 6 | 6.0 Estimated Study Hours
0/4 Done
Learning Objectives
  • Formulate strict, testable, and falsifiable product and SEO hypotheses.
  • Understand the scientific method in software development and marketing: Null hypothesis vs Alternative hypothesis.
  • Design experiment cards that specify primary metrics, guardrail metrics, and minimum test runtimes.
Core Concepts
  • Falsifiability: A claim is only scientific if there exists an observation that could prove it false.
  • The 3-Part Hypothesis Structure: Variable Changed + Expected Customer Behavior + Quantifiable Business Impact.
  • Confirmation Bias: The dangerous psychological tendency to search only for evidence confirming our pre-existing beliefs.

Week 9
A/B Testing, Confounding Factors & Before-and-After Limits
Competency 6 | 6.5 Estimated Study Hours
0/4 Done
Learning Objectives
  • Differentiate between user-split A/B testing (CRO) and page-cluster split testing (SEO).
  • Identify and control for confounding variables: seasonality, external marketing spend, PR spikes, and Google core updates.
  • Design an AI validation test suite to backtest diagnostic accuracy against historical cases.
Core Concepts
  • Confounding Bias: Attributing an organic lift to an SEO title change when Black Friday demand was the real driver.
  • Regression to the Mean: Understanding why extremely high or low traffic periods naturally normalize.
  • AI Diagnostic Precision & Recall: Measuring false positives (crying wolf on non-issues) vs false negatives (missing true drops).

Competency 7 | 6.5 Estimated Study Hours
