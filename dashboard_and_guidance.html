Junior Product Manager Readiness Evaluation

Objective assessment rubric based on demonstrated portfolio outputs rather than course completion certificates.

💡
How Senior Product Leaders Actually Evaluate You: Product leaders do not care how many courses you attended. They look for clarity of thought, comfort with data uncertainty, and humility in the face of customer feedback. Evaluate your readiness across the 5 core proof points below.
1. Problem Framing Without Solution Bias
Discovery Competency

The Test: Can you articulate a real user frustration and its business cost without mentioning "AI", "software", or specific features for at least 10 minutes?

Your Portfolio Evidence: Artifact 1 (Problem Statement) and Artifact 2 (5 User Interview Transcripts with neutral non-leading questions).
2. Diagnostic Rigor & Root Cause Isolation
Analytical Competency

The Test: When presented with a complex performance drop, do you jump to surface tweaks (rewriting meta tags) or do you segment data to isolate root causes?

Your Portfolio Evidence: Artifact 1 (Diagnostic Audit Report) and Artifact 6 (Deterministic Decision Framework ruling out seasonality and confounding updates).
3. Strategic Prioritization & Defending "No"
Strategy Competency

The Test: Can you defend an explicit "Won't Have" list against an excited stakeholder using transparent mathematical scoring (RICE) and business trade-offs?

Your Portfolio Evidence: Artifact 4 (MVP Scope Exclusions) and Artifact 3 (Ranked Pain Points Matrix).
4. Developer-Ready Technical Communication
Delivery Competency

The Test: Can a senior software engineer read your ticket without asking 10 basic clarification questions about edge cases, error states, and expected outputs?

Your Portfolio Evidence: Artifact 8 (Lightweight PRD with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria and Definition of Done).
5. Scientific Experimentation & Causal Attribution
Critical Thinking

The Test: Do you recognize that before-and-after graphs are not scientific proof of success, and can you design controlled tests that account for confounding variables?

Your Portfolio Evidence: Artifact 10 (AI Backtesting Plan) and Artifact 11 (Trust & Safety Risk Register).
