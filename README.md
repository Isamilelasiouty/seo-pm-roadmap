# SEO to Product Management: The Dual-Competency Roadmap 🚀

An interactive, evidence-based 12-week learning roadmap bridging Search Engine Optimization (SEO) with Product Management (PM) and AI product development.

## 🌟 Overview
Built for SEO professionals with ~2 years of experience who want to strengthen their technical SEO diagnostics while developing the transferable product competencies needed to transition into Product Management.

The roadmap centers around an early-stage AI product capstone: **"AI-Powered SEO Diagnostic Assistant: From Problem Discovery to Validated MVP"**.

## 🧠 The 7 Core Competencies
1. **Data Analysis & Problem Diagnosis:** Root cause analysis, Google Search Console anomaly detection, GSC + GA4 data triangulation, 5 Whys, and Fishbone diagrams.
2. **User Research & Search Intent:** Qualitative discovery interviews ("The Mom Test"), Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD), and intent modeling beyond keywords.
3. **Prioritization & Strategic Thinking:** Quantitative RICE scoring, MoSCoW scoping, opportunity sizing, and defining MVP boundaries.
4. **Metrics, KPIs & Product Analytics:** Outputs vs. outcomes, North Star Metric framework, KPI trees, and AARRR behavioral retention cohorts.
5. **User Experience (UX) & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO):** Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics, progressive disclosure, and diagnostic UI wireframing.
6. **Experimentation & Critical Thinking:** Falsifiable hypothesis testing, SEO split-testing across URL clusters, confounding factor isolation, and causal attribution.
7. **Technical Communication & Requirements:** Agile user stories, Gherkin (Given/When/Then) acceptance criteria, data schemas, and developer-ready PRDs.

## 🛠️ Key Features of the Interactive App
- **Dynamic Progress Engine:** Tracks 48 weekly milestones and 12 capstone artifacts.
- **Verified Resource Library:** 23 authenticated resources from Google Search Central, Atlassian, Nielsen Norman Group, Amplitude, and SQLBolt.
- **Interactive Search & Multi-Facet Filters:** Filter resources by skill, format, difficulty, and access.
- **Personal Learning Log & Notes:** Auto-saved study log and reflection areas using `localStorage`.
- **Professional English Integration:** Phase-by-phase vocabulary with phonetics, definitions, and stakeholder communication templates.
- **Print & PDF Export:** Clean print layout for generating portfolio PDFs.
